## CoFriends - An Interest-Group Based Social Platform

An interest-based social platform built to help people connect through shared interests and hopefully turn those connections into genuine, lasting friendships.

I originally built this as my final year project at Nanyang Technological University. After the project was completed, I continued working on it and explored the idea of turning it into something more than just a university project. Eventually, I decided to discontinue it — building a social platform turned out to be the easy part; convincing people to use yet another one was a much harder problem 😄

I'm keeping the project here as a snapshot of what I built and learned along the way.

Tech Stack:
HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Redux, Node, Express, Socket.IO, PostgreSQL, NGINX, AWS
